Brisket

Delicious cut of beef cooked low and slow

Texas barbecue brisket is a cut of beef from the lower chest or breast of a cow that's been smoked at a low temperature for a long time. It's known for its smoky flavor, tender meat, and distinctive crust.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole brisket, untrimmed, 10 to 12 pounds
  • 1/2 cup kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup coarse ground black pepper
  • 1/4 cup paprika
  • 1/4 cup garlic powder
  • 1/4 cup onion powder
  • 1/4 cup chili powder

Instructions

  1. Trim the fat cap on the brisket to 1/4 inch.
  2. Combine the salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and chili powder in a bowl.
  3. Season the brisket with the spice mixture.
  4. Smoke the brisket at 225°F for 12 to 20 hours.
  5. Rest the brisket for 1 hour before slicing.
